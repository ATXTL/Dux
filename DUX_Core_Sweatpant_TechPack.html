Construction & fit notes

1
Fit: wide-leg straight. Leg opening must equal knee measurement across all sizes. Zero taper from knee to hem — any visible narrowing at the ankle will be rejected.
2
Mid-rise positioning. Front rise 13″ (M). This is a deliberate mid-rise — do not lower the rise without written approval.
3
Inseam length 31″ (M). Intentionally long to produce a slight ankle stack. Do not shorten inseam without approval.
4
Brushed loop reverse. The inner face of the French Terry must be fully brushed. Unbrushed internal fabric will be rejected at inspection.
5
Pre-shrunk fabric. All fabric must be pre-shrunk before cutting. Include shrinkage test results with fit sample submission.
6
Waistband. 3″ ribbed waistband, clean front — no drawcord, no eyelets. Elastic waistband only. Confirm elastic tension with DUX at fit sample stage.
7
Label. No woven label. Printed care/size label inside waistband seam. Label artwork to be supplied by DUX separately.
8
Tonal stitching throughout. All visible stitching must be colour-matched to the Warm Slate Grey fabric. No contrasting thread visible on exterior seams.
